About

Erfan Saadi is a multi-instrument composer and songwriter, born in Tehran in March 1989.

He began on classical guitar at 17. Over time the electric guitar became his favorite instrument.

In 2010 he left Iran to study music and sound engineering for four years. In 2014 he formed the heavy metal band RUK in Tehran. His latest single in Farsi, “Sodaye To”, came out in 2025 and is his second song in the language.
