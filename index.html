    Biography

    About

    Erfan Saadi writes with a guitar in his hands and a recording desk in his head.

    Born in Tehran in March 1989, he is a multi-instrument composer and songwriter whose music moves between heavy riffs and the quiet of a single string. He came to the guitar at seventeen, starting on classical guitar. The electric guitar followed and soon became his favorite instrument.

    In 2010 he left Iran to study music and sound engineering for four years, learning to shape a song from both sides of the studio glass. In 2014 he formed the heavy metal band RUK in Tehran. He leads RUK Band as its founder and works as a music composer with RUK Studios.

    His own catalogue starts in 2013 and grows single by single: Walking on the Edge in 2014, Falling Song, Dream Big and Lost Island (with Eric Zang) in 2015, Talk To Me in 2018, Crazy Dream and Rising in 2020, Del Nava in 2024. In 2025 came Sodaye To, only his second song in Farsi, and then his latest, Endless Strings.

    Away from the stage he keeps a working collection of nine instruments, from an American Stratocaster to a seven-string Les Paul, and shares the work as it happens: sessions at WASP Studios, a guitar-shopping day in Istanbul, a night at OVO Bistro. Rukband, Persian Guitar Heroes and the Erfan Saadi Band all live alongside his solo music on Instagram.
